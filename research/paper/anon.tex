%%
%% anon.tex -- the ANONYMOUS (double-blind) build of the REFLEX paper.
%%
%% ICAIF '26 review is double-blind, so this is the PDF that goes to CMT.
%% It is not a second copy of the paper: it defines the \ANON switch and then
%% pulls in main.tex verbatim, so there is exactly one source of truth for the
%% text and the two builds cannot drift apart.
%%
%% What \ANON changes inside main.tex:
%%   - the class line becomes \documentclass[sigconf,anonymous,review]{acmart},
%%     so acmart collapses the author block to "Anonymous Author(s)", drops the
%%     affiliations, emails and author notes, and numbers the lines;
%%   - the Reproducibility statement drops the authors' GitHub URL.
%%
%% The line numbers come from `review` and are an author choice: the ICAIF '26
%% CFP asks only for "the 'sigconf' class with the 'anonymous' parameter" and
%% says nothing about line numbering either way. They are kept so reviewers can
%% point at a specific claim. The paper is 8 pages with or without them.
%%
%% ORCIDs are absent on purpose. An ORCID iD identifies its holder, so it
%% cannot appear in a double-blind submission. ACM requires ORCIDs from all
%% authors of ACCEPTED papers at camera-ready, which is the de-anonymised
%% build's job, not this one's.
%%
%% Build locally:            latexmk -pdf anon.tex
%% Build on Overleaf:        set anon.tex as the project's main document.
%%
%% Output is committed as REFLEX_Research_Paper_v4_ANONYMOUS_submission.pdf.
%% The de-anonymised working PDF is REFLEX_Research_Paper.pdf, built from
%% main.tex directly.
%%
